Tower Defense — Personal Unity Project

About the Project
This is a personal project I developed over the summer to gain a better understanding of the video game development process, from designing gameplay mechanics to implementing them in Unity.
I also wanted to experiment with gameplay ideas that I hadn't encountered in the games I had played.

Gameplay
The game is a tower defense in which players place all their turrets before the battle begins. Once the enemy waves start, they must defend their positions by using abilities and managing their available resources.
The gameplay revolves around two main mechanics:
1. Turret Disabling and Recovery
When enemies reach a turret, they can disable it, preventing it from attacking.
To restore a disabled turret, the player must first clear the surrounding area of enemies and then send supplies to reactivate it.
This creates situations where players must decide whether to focus on defending their remaining turrets or invest resources in recovering lost positions while the enemy waves continue.
2. Dynamic Turret Boosting
Players can boost a limited number of turrets at the same time.
These boosts can be reassigned at any point during the battle, allowing players to adapt their strategy as the situation changes.
For example, a player might temporarily boost several turrets to clear an area, send supplies to restore a disabled turret, and then redistribute the boosts to defend another position.

Opening the Project in Unity
Just in case you'd like to explore the project in Unity, first thank you for taking the time to check it out!
To start the game, open the Level Selector scene in Unity. This is the intended starting point for players.
